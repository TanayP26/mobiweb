Design a premium, modern, interactive website for **Mobilon Club, Bennett University**.

Mobilon is a technology and product-building student club focused on mobile development, software products, AI, design, hackathons, technical workshops, showcases and innovation.

The website should feel like the official digital home of a serious university technology community.

Do NOT design it like a generic student club website.

Do NOT make it look AI-generated.

The visual quality should feel comparable to a premium technology company, modern developer platform, creative studio or high-end university innovation lab.

---

Overall mood:

* premium
* technical
* confident
* editorial
* futuristic but restrained
* professional
* minimal
* visually memorable

Deep black / navy background with selective violet, indigo and electric-blue illumination.

## CRITICAL VISUAL RULE

Avoid typical AI-generated website patterns such as:

* giant gradient blobs everywhere
* endless glassmorphism
* every section inside rounded cards
* huge pill-shaped buttons
* floating decorative spheres
* random particles
* excessive glow
* neon gaming aesthetic
* crypto aesthetic
* 3D cards
* spinning objects
* typing animations
* giant dashboard-looking widgets
* repetitive 3-column icon cards

The site should feel deliberately designed by a strong UI/UX designer.

Premium quality should come from:

* typography
* whitespace
* grid composition
* strong hierarchy
* restrained lighting
* excellent photography
* subtle motion
* asymmetrical layouts
* spacing consistency
* microinteractions
